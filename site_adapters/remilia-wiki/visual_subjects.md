# Remilia Wiki visual-subject supplement

Use this supplement for artwork, character designs, NFT/PFP collections, cards, logos, comics, and other primarily visual subjects.

## Artifact description

A work displayed in the article is a primary artifact. After actually viewing it, an author may describe plainly visible subject matter, composition, pose, framing, color, medium when unambiguous, printed text, and recurring features. The description must be independently checkable against the shown work.

The artifact does not by itself establish intent, symbolism, influence, authorship, production process, release date, reception, popularity, supply, or significance. Those come from another inspected source or from the contributor's material ([Sources and evidence](editorial.md#sources-and-evidence)). When a contributor describes a work that the article shows, compare the description with the work and ask about any mismatch.

Show before describing. Captions identify and locate; they should not praise or interpret. Describe load-bearing distinctions rather than cataloguing every visible detail.

## Remilia-specific authorship

Where post-authorship applies, attribute a series to Remilia or the sourced collective entity rather than inventing an individual author. Name an individual only when an inspected source or the contributor credits them. Do not infer an individual author from style, access, or social proximity.

Terms such as network spirituality, dollcore, oekaki, and gijinka can be recorded as terminology. Claiming that a particular work belongs to, derives from, or intentionally references one of these traditions is interpretive: it needs a source or the contributor's material, and the agent does not infer it from resemblance.

## Structure and thinness

Potential sections include design and aesthetic, production, series or roster, influence and lineage, and reception. Include only sections supported by the artifact, the sources, and the contributor's material. If visual description yields one small paragraph and the rest would be inferred context, prefer a redirect or a section in a parent article.

Avoid art-criticism puffery, and resemblance, process or intent that neither a source nor the contributor states. A shorter description that the reader can verify is stronger than a speculative interpretation.
