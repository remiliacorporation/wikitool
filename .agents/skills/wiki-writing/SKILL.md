# Wiki writing

Produce a useful, source-bound article within the requested scope. This skill
owns research and prose judgment; Wikitool supplies retrieval, syntax checks,
and guarded publication. A draft request does not require publication.

For a frozen evaluation or delegated author packet, first read
[frozen-packet.md](references/frozen-packet.md). Its declared inputs replace
ambient project and network context.

## Establish the article

Read supplied materials before narrowing the subject or asking questions.
Resolve material ambiguity about identity, page type, and scope. Use
`wiki-interview` when important human knowledge or exclusions are missing;
reuse an adequate existing brief rather than repeating the interview.

In ordinary project work, inspect relevant adapter guidance and use
`article scout` when local integration facts are needed. Local pages, search
snippets, rank, and model memory are leads to inspect, not claim evidence.

## Write from inspected evidence

Map material claims to exact documents and entailing passages before drafting.
Preserve source role, dates, attribution, uncertainty, and scope. Primary
artifacts support what they directly establish; significance, reception, and
contested interpretation need appropriate independent support. Hold unsupported
central claims; omit unsupported optional claims without making them blockers.

Use [evidence-to-prose.md](references/evidence-to-prose.md) for claim mapping,
source selection, citation, and paraphrase decisions. When working from human
materials, also use [human-notes.md](references/human-notes.md).

Derive structure from the subject and evidence. Draft the factual body, then a
lead that identifies the subject and summarizes its supported center. Include
host-wiki relationships only when sourced, important, and proportionate.
Remove filler and unsupported synthesis because they fail the reader, not
because they match a phrase blacklist.

Use [mediawiki-structure.md](references/mediawiki-structure.md) for wikitext,
templates, categories, references, and article integration. Inspect real
template interfaces rather than guessing parameters. Lint the draft, inspect
any safe fixes, and resolve errors or explain remaining limitations.

## Review and delivery

For a completed ordinary article draft, obtain an independent `prose-review`
invocation using the exact candidate and sources, without author rationale or
an intended verdict. Address material findings and repeat review after
substantive changes. If independence is unavailable, deliver the draft with
that limit; self-review cannot certify independent acceptance.

Present the requested draft and its material evidence or review limitations.
For publication, a named human must read and accept the exact final prose.
Never record that decision on the human's behalf without their actual
acceptance. Hash-bound acceptance is invalidated by subsequent prose changes.
Use the Wikitool sync lane when promotion or remote publication is requested.
Frozen author packets end at their declared outputs; do not simulate downstream
review, human acceptance, or publication.
